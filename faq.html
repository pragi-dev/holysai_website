FAQ

Quick answers to common questions from parents.

What is the educational philosophy of Holy Sai Preparatory School?

We are dedicated to nurturing responsible, confident and compassionate individuals. Our approach prioritises unpressurised learning, respecting each child’s unique pace, and focuses on intellectual, social, emotional and physical development.

How important is a preparatory school for a community?

Preparatory schools provide quality education, foster character development, promote community engagement, nurture leadership and create pathways to higher education — with individualised attention tailored to each student and access to better resources.

What co-scholastic activities are offered?

Art & Craft, Instrumental (Drums & Keyboard), Life Skills, Dance, Sports, Yoga & Meditation, Robotics, Theatre and Karate.

What safety and security measures does the school follow?

A qualified medical officer and equipped medical room, regular health check-ups, government health protocols, CCTV across campus, GPS in buses, experienced drivers and attenders, and visitors by prior appointment only.

Are your certifications globally recognised?

Yes, our certifications are globally respected, providing international opportunities and enhancing credibility for our students.

How can I contact the school for admissions?

Call +91 99404 30003 or email info@holysaiprepschool.org.in. We are open Monday to Saturday, 09.00 AM – 05.00 PM.
