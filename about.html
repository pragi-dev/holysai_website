Welcome to the
Holy Sai Family

2007

Pioneering milestone

The Holy Sai Group of Institutions began in Chennai — and in 2024 arrived in Coimbatore with Holy Sai Preparatory School, Vadavalli.

Nurturing intellect, heart and character

Holy Sai Schools are the vision of the members of the Shiridi Sai Educational & Charitable Trust to provide quality education with global standards.

In this Man Making Mission towards creating leaders for the next generation, we emerge as a school with a difference — focusing on child-centred education and creating an environment for students to “Learn Beyond Limits”.

☰ Chairman’s desk

“Bringing the best out of each student”

Holy Sai Group of Institutions aims in bringing the best out of each student by imparting holistic and value education. In our pursuit of excellence, we persistently seek and adopt innovations to improve the quality of education.

President of the Tamil Nadu Association of Cambridge Schools (TACS) and Secretary of UDSFSA, Dr. Prem Shankar led the Holy Sai R&D team in training over 300 teachers through Ignite 23 – the Educator’s Edition, and champions sustainable living through the Outdoor Learning Centre at Pollachi.

S. Prem Shankar

Our success journey

From Chennai to Coimbatore

  1. 2007

    Holy Sai International School

    Flagship school established at Guduvanchery, Chennai.

  2. 2013

    First IGCSE batch

    The only school between Tambaram and Chengalpattu to offer Cambridge IGCSE.

  3. 2019

    AS & A Levels

    First batch of Cambridge AS (Class XI) and A Level (Class XII).

  4. 2022

    Annex blocks

    Annex 1 & 2, a multipurpose theatre hall and a spacious courtyard.

  5. 2023

    Outdoor Learning Centre

    Opened at Pollachi with the motto ‘Mann, Maram, Makkal’.

  6. 2024

    Holy Sai arrives @ Coimbatore

    Holy Sai Preparatory School opens at Vadavalli.
