Safety & security

A Safe Day at Holy Sai

Every hour of your child’s day is looked after — here’s how.

  1. 🚌
    Morning arrival

    GPS-tracked buses with experienced drivers and attenders bring children to school.

  2. 🔐
    At the gate

    Visitors are allowed only with a prior appointment.

  3. 📹
    Through the day

    CCTV monitoring across the campus and BotsEye surveillance.

  4. 🩺
    If anyone feels unwell

    A qualified medical officer, a medical room and regular health check-ups.

  5. 🧼
    Clean spaces

    Washrooms hygienically maintained with Bosch machines.

  6. 🏠
    Home time

    The same GPS-tracked buses and attenders take every child safely home.
