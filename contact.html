Quick answers

Before You Get in Touch

A few things parents often ask us.

All FAQs →
How can I contact the school for admissions?

Call +91 99404 30003 or email info@holysaiprepschool.org.in. We are open Monday to Saturday, 09.00 AM – 05.00 PM.

Which classes are open for admission?

Admissions are open for the 2027–28 academic year, Pre KG – Grade IX, from 14 November. You can also send an enquiry from the Admissions page.

Can I visit the campus?

Yes — visitors are welcome by prior appointment. Call or email the office to choose a time.

What safety measures does the school follow?

A qualified medical officer and equipped medical room, regular health check-ups, government health protocols, CCTV across campus, GPS in buses, experienced drivers and attenders, and visitors by prior appointment only.
